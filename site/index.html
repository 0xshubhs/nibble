how it works

Three steps, then forget it exists.

1 · No installer, no account

Mount the .dmg, run the portable .exe, or chmod +x the AppImage. The mark appears in your menu bar and stays there.

2 · Turn on a source

Clipboard, folders you nominate, or a hotkey you press on purpose. Everything is chunked and indexed locally: searchable the moment it lands, embedded a beat later.

3 · Paste one line

Add the connector to anything that speaks MCP. From then on it looks things up instead of asking you to explain again.

what it does

A memory that stays on your machine.

Words and meaning, fused

Keyword and semantic search run together and are combined by rank, not by score. Below a similarity floor it says no results, instead of returning everything in some order.

Indexed on your machine

A 23 MB model runs in its own process, downloaded once and offline afterwards. Text is searchable the instant it is captured; the vectors fill in behind it.

Follow a thought sideways

Every result opens its nearest neighbours: what else you captured that sits close to it in meaning, even when it shares no words. Your assistant gets the same move as a tool.

Capture you can see

Clipboard, folders, and a global hotkey for one deliberate save. Every source has a switch, a count, and a global pause. Anything shaped like a password is dropped before it is written.

Reminders that reach you

Real system notifications. Once, hourly, daily, weekdays or weekly, re-checked when the machine wakes, so something that came due in your bag still arrives.

Forget, properly

Forget one result, or everything a source ever captured. Compact rewrites the files without it. No account means no copy anywhere else to chase.

connect your llm

One line, then your AI can just ask.

Nibble speaks MCP, so any client that takes a connector can search your memory as a tool, follow a result to what is near it, and schedule reminders while it is there.

claude mcp add nibble -- node /path/to/nibble/mcp/stdio.js
search_memory related_memory recent_memory remember memory_stats list_reminders add_reminder

The server binds to 127.0.0.1, every request needs a bearer token, and the Origin header is checked, because anything running on your machine, including a web page you have open, can also reach localhost.
